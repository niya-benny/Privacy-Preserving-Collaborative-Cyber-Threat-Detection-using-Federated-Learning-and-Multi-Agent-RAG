Institution_A incident response playbook for Infiltration. Escalate to the NOC duty officer (ext 4100). Target recovery time: 30 minutes. Temporary blocks of up to 1 hour may be applied automatically by the NOC.

- Isolate the host immediately
- Preserve disk and memory evidence
- Reset credentials used on the host
- Escalate to the incident response lead and scan connected hosts
