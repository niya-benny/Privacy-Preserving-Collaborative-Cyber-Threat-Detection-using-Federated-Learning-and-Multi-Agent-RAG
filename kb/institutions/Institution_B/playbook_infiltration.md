Institution_B incident response playbook for Infiltration. Escalate to the CISO office (ext 5200). Target recovery time: 1 hour. Every block must be approved by the CISO before it is applied.

- Isolate the host immediately
- Preserve disk and memory evidence
- Reset credentials used on the host
- Escalate to the incident response lead and scan connected hosts
