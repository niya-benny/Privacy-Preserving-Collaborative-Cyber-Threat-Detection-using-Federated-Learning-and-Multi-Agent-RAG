Institution_B incident response playbook for DDoS. Escalate to the CISO office (ext 5200). Target recovery time: 1 hour. Every block must be approved by the CISO before it is applied.

- Notify the upstream provider and request traffic filtering
- Enable SYN-flood protection and rate limits
- Switch critical services to the backup path
- Post a status notice for users
