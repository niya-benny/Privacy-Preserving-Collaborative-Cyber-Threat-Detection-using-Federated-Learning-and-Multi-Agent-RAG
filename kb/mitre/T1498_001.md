MITRE ATT&CK T1498.001 Direct Network Flood. Adversaries may attempt to cause a denial of service (DoS) by directly sending a high-volume of network traffic to a target. This DoS attack may also reduce the availability and functionality of the targeted system(s) and network. Direct Network Floods are when one or more systems are used to send a high-volume of network packets towards the targeted service's network. Almost any network protocol may be used for flooding.

Tactic: impact. Mitigations listed by MITRE: Filter Network Traffic (M1037).
