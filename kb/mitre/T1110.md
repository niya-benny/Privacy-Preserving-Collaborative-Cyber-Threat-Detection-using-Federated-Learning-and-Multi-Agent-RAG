MITRE ATT&CK T1110 Brute Force. Adversaries may use brute force techniques to gain access to accounts when passwords are unknown or when password hashes are obtained. Without knowledge of the password for an account or set of accounts, an adversary may systematically guess the password using a repetitive or iterative mechanism.

Tactic: credential access. Mitigations listed by MITRE: Account Use Policies (M1036), Multi-factor Authentication (M1032), Password Policies (M1027), User Account Management (M1018).
