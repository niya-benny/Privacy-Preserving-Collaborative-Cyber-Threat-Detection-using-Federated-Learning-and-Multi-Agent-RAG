MITRE ATT&CK T1110.003 Password Spraying. Adversaries may use a single or small list of commonly used passwords against many different accounts to attempt to acquire valid account credentials. Password spraying uses one password (e.g. 'Password01'), or a small list of commonly used passwords, that may match the complexity policy of the domain.

Tactic: credential access. Mitigations listed by MITRE: Account Use Policies (M1036), Multi-factor Authentication (M1032), Password Policies (M1027).
