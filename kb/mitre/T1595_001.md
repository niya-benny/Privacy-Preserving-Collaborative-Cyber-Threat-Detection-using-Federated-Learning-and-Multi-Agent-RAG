MITRE ATT&CK T1595.001 Scanning IP Blocks. Adversaries may scan victim IP blocks to gather information that can be used during targeting. Public IP addresses may be allocated to organizations by block, or a range of sequential addresses.

Tactic: reconnaissance. Mitigations listed by MITRE: Pre-compromise (M1056).
