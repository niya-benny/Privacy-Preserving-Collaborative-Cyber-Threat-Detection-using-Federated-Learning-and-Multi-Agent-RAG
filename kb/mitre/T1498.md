MITRE ATT&CK T1498 Network Denial of Service. Adversaries may perform Network Denial of Service (DoS) attacks to degrade or block the availability of targeted resources to users. Network DoS can be performed by exhausting the network bandwidth services rely on. Example resources include specific websites, email services, DNS, and web-based applications.

Tactic: impact. Mitigations listed by MITRE: Filter Network Traffic (M1037).
